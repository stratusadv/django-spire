## Purpose

Make Django application development modular, extensible, and configurable.

## Why Django Spire

- **Authentication & Access** — user and group management with MFA, hashed API keys, and group-based permissions built in.
- **Unified Notifications** — email, SMS, push, and in-app messages through one pipeline, with automations and per-channel processors.
- **AI Conversations** — chat and SMS assistants wired through Dandy, with intent routing to direct users around your app.
- **Help Desk** — full-featured ticketing with priorities, notifications, and a service layer ready to extend.
- **History & Audit** — soft deletes, history events, and automatic activity attribution on every save.
- **Metrics & Reports** — reports, charts, and live statistics assembled from your models.
